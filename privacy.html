Nok Autopost: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026.

Nok Autopost is a private publishing tool used by the owner of the Instagram account @noknyc to schedule and publish that account's own posts. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data it uses

The tool uses one access token, granted by the account owner, to publish the owner's own photos and videos to @noknyc and to read that account's own post statistics. It does not collect, store, sell or share personal data about anyone else. It does not read messages or comments.

Data deletion

To revoke access and delete any data held by the tool, remove "Nok Autopost-IG" in Instagram: Settings, Website permissions, Apps and websites, Active, Remove. The tool keeps no personal data of its own; removing access ends all use. Questions: contact the account owner through the @noknyc Instagram profile.
